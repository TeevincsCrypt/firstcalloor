How it works

Every timeline starts from the token's real on-chain birth, never a third-party's self-reported timestamp. From there, FIRSTCALLOOR searches for the exact contract address — not the ticker — and filters out recycled CAs, retweets, and bot accounts before anyone gets ranked as first.
